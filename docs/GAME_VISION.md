# Game Vision

## High Concept

A browser-based GFL2 action roguelite with real-time gunplay, character-specific builds, randomized stage objectives, mechanically distinct bosses, and instant switching between third-person and elevated top-down combat.

## Design Pillars

1. Gunplay and movement must feel good before content expansion.
2. Two cameras present the same combat simulation; neither grants hidden statistical advantages.
3. A Doll starts with her signature weapon and passive, then earns Skill 1, Skill 2, and Ultimate during the run.
4. Minimal maps look intentional through terrain, lighting, weather, horizon design, audio, and readable combat effects.
5. Skills, weapon growth, attachments, and Sardis create separate but understandable decision layers.
6. Bosses use telegraphs, phases, weak points, break windows, and stage-related mechanics.
7. Characters, weapons, upgrades, enemies, bosses, drops, and stages remain data-driven.

## Complete Run Loop

1. Open the main menu with a real-time rotating preview of the selected stage.
2. Choose a Doll and begin Stage 1.
3. Start with the signature firearm and passive only.
4. Kill enemies for EXP, Sardis, and attachment drops.
5. Pause at level-up and choose one of three upgrade cards.
6. Find the randomized activation pedestal.
7. Activate it when ready and survive the boss event.
8. Defeat the boss, reveal the extraction pod, manage loot, and spend Sardis.
9. Travel to the next stage.
10. Defeat the Winter boss to complete the initial four-stage run.

Death ends the run. Permanent account progression, login, cloud saves, and multiplayer are deferred.

## Initial One-Shot Goal

Deliver a complete Grassland vertical slice with Tololo, both cameras, combat, progression, enemies, attachments, Sardis, a randomized pedestal, a multi-phase boss, extraction, pause/death/retry, tests, and performance diagnostics.

The one-shot is an extensible gameplay foundation, not the final four-stage game and not a claim of final visual quality.
